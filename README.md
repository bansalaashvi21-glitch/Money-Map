# Money Map

### Mapping your money. Understanding your financial picture.

**Money Map** is a Python-based financial analysis tool designed to help users understand their income, expenses, savings, and financial growth.

The tool takes basic financial information and converts it into meaningful insights, making financial data easier to understand, analyse, and interpret.

## Purpose

Managing finances involves more than simply recording expenses. Understanding how much is earned, how much is spent, how much is saved, and how savings can grow over time provides a clearer picture of a household's financial position.

Money Map was created to make this analysis simple, structured, and accessible through programming.

## What Money Map Does

Money Map allows users to:

* Enter and analyse income and expense data
* Calculate total savings
* Calculate the percentage of income saved
* Analyse household spending
* Organise financial information for easier interpretation
* Calculate compound growth over time
* Generate a consolidated financial overview

## Key Features

### Income and Expense Analysis

Money Map compares income and expenses to determine how much money remains after spending.

### Savings Analysis

The tool calculates total savings and the savings rate, helping users understand how much of their income is being retained.

### Expense Analysis

Financial data can be examined to understand spending patterns and identify major areas of expenditure.

### Compounding

Money Map includes compound growth calculations to demonstrate how savings or investments can grow over a chosen period based on user-defined assumptions.

### Financial Summary

The different calculations are brought together into a simple financial overview, allowing users to understand their overall financial position more easily.

## Example

For a household with a monthly income of **₹205,000** and monthly expenses of **₹157,841**, the remaining savings would be **₹47,159**.

This corresponds to a savings rate of approximately **23% of monthly income**.

The same financial information can then be used for further calculations and long-term growth projections.
